## 2. Description of Service

YIJI is a precision tap-counter application that records and analyzes tap events in real time. Core features include:

- **Real-time tap counting** — track every tap with millisecond precision
- **Interval statistics** — view average, minimum, maximum, and total session duration
- **Session history** — review past tap sessions with detailed timestamps
- **Cloud synchronization** — sync data across sessions when signed in with a Google account

**Free users** may access basic counting and a limited number of historical records.

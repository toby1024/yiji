## Billing & Renewal

- All payments are processed securely by **Google Play Billing**.
- Subscriptions **automatically renew** at the end of each billing period.
- You will be charged through your Google Play account on file.
- To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least **24 hours before** the renewal date.
- Renewal prices are the same as the initial subscription price, unless you are notified otherwise.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will I lose my data if I cancel?**  
A: No. All your tap session records remain intact. Free-tier users can view their most recent 5 sessions. Re-subscribing restores full history access immediately.

**Q: Can I switch between plans?**  
A: Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time through the app or Google Play. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**  
A: A free trial may be offered to new subscribers at our discretion. If available, it will be clearly indicated on the subscription screen before purchase.

**Q: What happens if my payment fails?**  
A: Google Play will retry the payment. If it continues to fail, your subscription will lapse and your account will revert to the Free plan. Your data is never deleted.

**Q: Are prices the same worldwide?**  
A: Prices are listed in USD. The amount charged may vary slightly based on your local currency, regional taxes (such as VAT or GST), and Google Play's local pricing policies.
